About Me

I am a Software Engineer and Computer Science student at the University of Central Florida, specializing in hardware-to-software integration, interactive systems, and simulation.

Currently, I am a Software Engineering Intern at NASA's Kennedy Space Center, supporting the Artemis Mission and Exploration Ground Systems. Working within the Advanced Imaging and Analysis Laboratory, I develop custom Python and ROS-based camera controllers for the SPIDR tracking system to capture critical rocket launch telemetry and imaging.

Previously, I served as an Assistant Scholar at Limbitless Solutions, using C++ and Unreal Engine to engineer training games that interface directly with electromyography (EMG) sensors for bionic limb patients. Along with my background developing VR simulations for the UCF Institute for Simulation and Training, my work is driven by a passion for translating complex physical data into functional, interactive software.
